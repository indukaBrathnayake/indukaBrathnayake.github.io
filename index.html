About Me

I'm an Electrical and Electronic Engineering undergraduate at the University of Peradeniya. Currently a third-year student, focused on deepening my technical expertise and taking on the advanced challenges of my engineering curriculum.

I am specializing in power, energy and high-voltage engineering driven by a strong interest in AI and machine learning. I love the hands-on process of circuit design, building, innovating and always seeking to develop smart systems.

Outside of the lab and lectures, I keep things balanced through my love for table tennis and hiking. Whether I’m leading my team in a fast-paced match on the court or heading out for a trek in the hills, these activities give me the discipline and fresh energy I need to stay sharp for my engineering career.
