Overview

Project Background & Objectives

This final year project focuses on developing an intelligent protection system for modern power distribution networks that integrate distributed generation. In Phase 1, the work is entirely centered on software and simulation, utilizing PSCAD/EMTDC and PSS/E to model standard IEEE test feeders. By simulating diverse fault conditions, we collect comprehensive voltage and current time-series data to train advanced deep learning model of Hybrid CNN-LSTM and Transformers—aiming for over 98% accuracy in fault classification.

Traditional impedance-based protection methods fail under modern grid conditions. This research aims to bridge that gap by training intelligent relay models on simulated fault waveform data generated from a MATLAB Simulink test feeder network.
